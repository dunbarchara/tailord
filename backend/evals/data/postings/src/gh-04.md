## About Datadogo
We're on a mission to bring high-quality monitoring and security to every engineering team. Datadogo is a SaaS platform trusted by tens of thousands of organizations, and our engineering teams ship to production hundreds of times a day.

## The team
Incident Management helps on-call engineers detect, triage and resolve problems quickly. We build the APIs, background workers and integrations behind paging, status pages and postmortems.

## At Datadogo, you will
- Build and operate Python services that handle tens of thousands of requests per second.
- Design PostgreSQL schemas and queries that stay fast as our customers' data grows.
- Develop asynchronous workers on top of queues to deliver notifications reliably.
- Write unit, integration and load tests, and be thoughtful about rollout and rollback.
- Be part of an on-call rotation for the services you build.
- Work with product designers and managers on new features.

## Requirements
- 3+ years of professional experience building backend services, preferably in Python.
- Solid understanding of relational databases, especially PostgreSQL.
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Experience with a major cloud provider such as AWS.
- Willingness to learn Go, which we use for latency-critical paths.

## Bonus points
- Experience with Kafka, Redis or other messaging systems.
- Experience with Terraform or Kubernetes.
- Contributions to open source projects.
