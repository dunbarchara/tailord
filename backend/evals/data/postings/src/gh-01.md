## About the job
Googleo's Cloud Storage team builds the infrastructure that keeps exabytes of customer data durable, fast and cheap. As a Senior Software Engineer you will design and operate backend services that sit between our public APIs and the storage fleet, working with teammates across metadata, replication and billing.

## Responsibilities
- Design, build and launch backend services in Java and C++ that serve millions of requests per second.
- Own the reliability of your services: define SLOs, carry the pager in a shared rotation, and lead blameless postmortems.
- Partner with product managers and SRE to turn customer problems into technical roadmaps.
- Review code and design documents, and mentor engineers earlier in their careers.
- Improve developer productivity by investing in tooling, testing and release automation.

## Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 5 years of experience with software development in one or more programming languages, and with data structures and algorithms.
- 3 years of experience testing, maintaining or launching software products, and 1 year of experience with software design and architecture.

## Preferred qualifications
- Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Experience with distributed systems, consensus protocols or large-scale databases.
- Experience developing accessible technologies.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes or Borg-style cluster schedulers.

## About Googleo Cloud
Googleo Cloud helps organizations of every size digitally transform their business. Our customers in more than 200 countries use our products to modernize how they build, store and analyze.
