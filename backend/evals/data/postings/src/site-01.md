Supabaso is the open source Postgres development platform. We give every developer a production-grade database, authentication, storage and realtime in minutes. We're a fully remote team of about 90 people, serving more than a million developers.

## The role
We're looking for a Site Reliability Engineer to help us keep millions of Postgres databases fast and available. You'll work across our fleet management, observability and incident response, and you'll have a real say in how we run our infrastructure.

## What you'll be doing
- Operate and improve the platform that provisions, upgrades and backs up customer Postgres databases on AWS.
- Build and maintain infrastructure as code with Terraform and Kubernetes manifests.
- Improve our observability stack and define SLOs with the engineering teams that own each service.
- Lead incident response, write postmortems, and drive the follow-up work to completion.
- Automate away toil, from capacity planning to certificate rotation.
- Be on call in a follow-the-sun rotation with the rest of the team.

## What we're looking for
- 5+ years of experience in SRE, DevOps or infrastructure engineering roles.
- Deep, hands-on knowledge of PostgreSQL operations: replication, backups, vacuum, connection pooling.
- Production experience with Kubernetes and a major cloud provider (AWS preferred).
- Proficiency in at least one programming language, such as Go, Python or Rust.
- Strong written communication. We are remote and async by default.

## It would be a bonus if you
- Have contributed to Postgres or related open source projects.
- Have run multi-tenant platforms at scale.
- Have experience with eBPF or low-level performance tooling.

## What we offer
Fully remote with a flexible schedule, competitive salary adjusted by region, meaningful equity, a $1,000 home office budget, and a full set of co-working and travel allowances. We also pay for the conferences you want to attend.

## Our process
A 30-minute intro call, a take-home exercise (about two hours, paid), a technical conversation with the team, and a final interview with a founder. We aim to move from application to offer in under three weeks.
