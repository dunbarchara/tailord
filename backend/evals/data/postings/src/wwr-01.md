GitLabo is the intelligent orchestration platform for DevSecOps. More than 50 million registered users and more than half of the Fortune 100 trust GitLabo to ship better, more secure software faster. We are an all-remote company: every team member works from where they are most productive.

As an Intermediate Backend Engineer on the Knowledge Graph team, you'll help build and operate a graph data service that powers search, analytics and AI features across the platform. You'll write production code, improve reliability and help shape how we model engineering data at scale.

## What you'll do
- Build and maintain backend services in Ruby on Rails and Go that ingest, store and query graph data.
- Design database schemas and queries, and tune performance on PostgreSQL and a graph store.
- Write automated tests and documentation, and review the code of your teammates.
- Participate in our on-call rotation and contribute to incident reviews.
- Collaborate asynchronously with product managers and engineers around the world.

## What you'll need
- 3+ years of professional experience as a backend engineer.
- Proficiency in Ruby, Go or another server-side language, and willingness to learn the others.
- Experience with relational databases and query optimization.
- Experience with Git and code review workflows.
- Excellent written communication in English.
- Ability to use GitLabo to build GitLabo.

## Nice to have
- Experience with graph databases or search technologies such as Elasticsearch.
- Knowledge of Kubernetes and cloud deployment.
- Familiarity with LLMs or retrieval pipelines.

## About the team
Our team is distributed across seven time zones. We practice handbook-first documentation and async communication, and we try to keep meetings to a minimum.
