## About the Role
We're hiring our first Engineering Manager to lead a team of 6 engineers building the core platform behind Replito: sandboxed code execution, realtime collaboration and deploys. You will split your time between people management, technical direction and hands-on work when the team needs it. 🚀

## What You'll Do
- Hire, grow and retain a high-performing team of backend and infrastructure engineers.
- Run planning and make sure we ship the right things, on time, with high quality.
- Partner with product and design to define the roadmap for the platform.
- Set and uphold the engineering bar: code review, testing, incident response.
- Coach engineers through career growth, feedback and performance conversations.
- Write code, review designs and unblock the team when it is useful. About 20% of your time.

## What We're Looking For
- 3+ years managing engineers, and 6+ years as a software engineer.
- Experience scaling a backend platform from early stage; Python, Go or TypeScript.
- Strong grasp of cloud infrastructure and containers.
- Track record of hiring well and handling underperformance with kindness and clarity.
- Excellent communication, async-friendly.

## Nice To Have
- Experience with sandboxing, virtualization or developer tooling.
- Experience in a startup under 100 people.
