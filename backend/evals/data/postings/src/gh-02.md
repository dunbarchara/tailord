## About Stripeo
Stripeo builds economic infrastructure for the internet. Businesses of every size, from new startups to public companies, use our software to accept payments, send payouts and manage their businesses online. We move trillions of dollars a year and care a great deal about correctness.

## About the team
The Payments Platform team owns the ledger, the idempotency layer and the state machines that every money movement flows through. We are a team of 14 engineers working mostly in Ruby and Java, with a growing number of services in Go.

## What you'll do
- Set the technical direction for how payment state is modeled, stored and reconciled across our services.
- Lead multi-quarter projects that span several teams, including migrations of PostgreSQL-backed services to our sharded data platform.
- Write and review design documents; raise the bar on API design, testing and operational readiness.
- Be an escalation point for production incidents and drive follow-up work so they don't recur.
- Mentor senior engineers and help grow the next generation of technical leads.

## Who you are
We're looking for someone who has:
- 10+ years of professional software engineering experience, including significant time leading technical projects.
- Deep experience designing and operating high-throughput transactional systems with strong consistency requirements.
- Strong command of at least one of Ruby, Java or Go, and comfort reading code in the others.
- Expert knowledge of relational databases, including schema design, indexing and online migrations.
- A track record of influencing without authority and of writing clearly.

## Nice to haves
- Experience with financial systems, double-entry ledgers or payments networks.
- Familiarity with Kafka or other event streaming platforms.
- Prior experience as a tech lead or staff engineer.

## Pay and benefits
The annual salary range for this role is $212,000 - $318,000 USD for US-based candidates. Salary is based on location and level; this role is also eligible for equity and benefits. We offer a home-office stipend, wellness benefits and 401(k) matching.
