## Overview
Nvidiao is looking for a Senior Systems Software Engineer to join our GPU driver team. You will work on the low-level software that lets every application, from games to data centers, talk to our hardware.

## What you will be doing
- Design and implement kernel-mode and user-mode driver features for Linux and Windows.
- Debug complex hardware and software interactions using kernel debuggers and hardware traces.
- Collaborate with hardware architects to bring up new silicon.
- Improve performance and power efficiency of the driver stack.
- Review code and mentor junior engineers.

## What we need to see
- BS or MS in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or related field (or equivalent experience).
- 6+ years of experience in systems programming with C or C++.
- Strong understanding of operating system internals, memory management and concurrency.
- Experience debugging at the kernel level.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and collaboration.

## Ways to stand out from the crowd
- Experience with GPU architecture, CUDA or graphics APIs.
- Background in device driver development.
- Knowledge of PCIe and DMA.

## Compensation
Your base salary will be determined based on your location, experience, and the pay of employees in similar positions. The base salary range is 184,000 USD - 356,500 USD for Level 4, and 224,000 USD - 425,500 USD for Level 5. You will also be eligible for equity and benefits.
