## About the role
The Editor Performance team keeps Figmao fast for the people who push it hardest: designers working in files with hundreds of thousands of layers. As a Senior Frontend Engineer you'll profile, diagnose and fix performance problems across our TypeScript, WebAssembly and WebGL stack.

## What you'll do
- Find and fix rendering, memory and interaction bottlenecks in a browser-based design editor.
- Build tooling and dashboards that track performance regressions across releases.
- Work in C++ compiled to WebAssembly where it matters, and TypeScript and React elsewhere.
- Partner with product engineers to set performance budgets for new features.
- Share what you learn through internal talks and documentation.

## We'd love to hear from you if you have
- 6+ years of experience building complex, performance-sensitive web applications.
- Deep expertise with JavaScript or TypeScript, browser internals and the rendering pipeline.
- Experience with profiling tools such as Chrome DevTools, flame graphs and tracing.
- Familiarity with WebGL, WebGPU or canvas rendering is a strong plus.
- Excellent communication and a data-driven approach.

## Nice to have
- Experience with C++, Rust or WebAssembly.
- Experience with multiplayer or CRDT-based collaborative systems.
