Basecampo has been building calm, profitable software for more than twenty years. We're a small company that ships a few products used by millions of people, and we like it that way.

WE'RE HIRING A SENIOR PROGRAMMER

You'll join our Product team to build and improve the products customers use every day. You'll write Ruby on Rails code, design features end to end and help us keep our codebases pleasant to work in.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Write clean, readable, well-tested Ruby on Rails code.
- Design and ship features with a designer and one other programmer.
- Take part in code review and give honest, kind feedback.
- Maintain and improve our existing apps.
- Share your work in writing, like our internal posts and public blog.

## WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
- 7+ years of professional programming, with at least 3+ in Ruby on Rails.
- A strong sense of taste for simple, durable design.
- Excellent written English. We work asynchronously and we write a lot.
- Experience with MySQL or PostgreSQL.
- You don't need a degree.

## NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with Hotwire, Stimulus or Turbo.
- Open source contributions.

## THE PERKS
- Salary: $220,000 flat, whatever your seniority or location
- Four-day work weeks from May through August
- Great health benefits and a 401(k) with a 6% match
- Sabbatical every three years
- A CHOICE of a $1,500 learning or fitness fund
