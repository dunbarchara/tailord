## Who we are
Notiono is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams and large companies run their work on Notiono.

## About the team
The Developer Platform team builds the APIs, SDKs and integrations that let customers and partners connect their tools to Notiono. AI Connections is our newest group, focused on letting agents securely read from and act in third-party apps.

## What you'll achieve
- Build and maintain the services that power connections between Notiono and external systems, including OAuth flows, webhooks and sync pipelines.
- Design public APIs that are consistent, well-documented and pleasant to use.
- Work with the AI team to expose tools to agents in a safe, auditable way.
- Improve the reliability and observability of connection infrastructure.
- Collaborate with design, product and support to make integrations feel native.

## Skills you'll need to bring
- 3+ years of software engineering experience, with strong TypeScript and Node.js skills.
- Experience building and consuming REST or GraphQL APIs.
- Familiarity with OAuth 2.0 and secure handling of third-party credentials.
- Experience with PostgreSQL or a similar relational database.
- A product mindset and the ability to work through ambiguity.

## Nice to haves
- Experience building developer platforms or public APIs with external users.
- Experience with LLM tool calling or agent frameworks.
- Prior work at a company with a large customer base of non-technical users.
