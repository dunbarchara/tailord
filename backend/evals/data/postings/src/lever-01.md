Netflixo is one of the world's leading entertainment services with over 280 million paid memberships in more than 190 countries. Our Content Analytics team helps decide what the world watches next by giving studio and finance partners trustworthy, timely data.

## The role
As a Senior Data Engineer you will build and operate the pipelines that turn billions of viewing events into the metrics that content teams rely on. You'll work in a culture of freedom and responsibility, where you are trusted to make high-impact decisions.

## What you will do
- Design and build batch and streaming pipelines using Spark, Flink and Python.
- Model data in a lakehouse and expose well-documented, tested datasets to analysts and data scientists.
- Own data quality: build monitoring and alerting so we find problems before our stakeholders do.
- Partner with analytics engineers and product managers to define metrics and their lineage.
- Contribute to our internal data platform by building reusable libraries and templates.

## What we are looking for
- 6+ years of experience building data pipelines at scale.
- Expert-level SQL and strong Python skills.
- Hands-on experience with Spark and a workflow orchestrator such as Airflow or similar.
- Experience with cloud data warehouses and object storage (AWS preferred).
- Strong communication skills and a collaborative approach.

## Bonus points
- Experience with Apache Iceberg or Delta Lake.
- Knowledge of Scala or Java.
- Background in media, entertainment, or consumer subscription analytics.

## Our culture
You can read about our culture memo at the link on our careers site. We are a diverse company and look for people who are curious, candid and highly collaborative.

## Compensation
Our compensation structure consists solely of an annual salary; we do not have bonuses. You choose each year how much of your compensation you want in salary versus stock options. The range for this role is $190,000 - $600,000.
