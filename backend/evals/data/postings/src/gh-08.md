## Job Description
To get the best candidate experience, please consider applying for a maximum of 3 roles within 12 months to ensure you are able to be fully considered for all roles.

Salesforco is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Our team builds the multi-tenant platform that thousands of customers customize with objects, workflows and code.

## Your impact
- Architect, design and build scalable, resilient services in Java on a multi-tenant platform.
- Lead the technical design of new features, from API contracts through data model to rollout.
- Drive engineering excellence through code reviews, design reviews and testing.
- Identify and resolve production issues, and improve the observability of your services.
- Collaborate with product managers, UX designers and fellow engineers across time zones.
- Coach and mentor junior engineers.

## Required skills
- 6+ years of backend software development experience.
- Strong object-oriented programming skills in Java.
- Experience with multi-tenant SaaS architectures.
- Experience with relational databases, SQL and performance tuning.
- Familiarity with Salesforco platform technologies such as Apex is a plus.
- Excellent analytical, communication and collaboration skills.

## Preferred skills
- Experience with Kubernetes and public cloud platforms.
- Experience with event-driven architectures and Kafka.
