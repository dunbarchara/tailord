## Overview
Do you want to build the systems that power one of the largest clouds in the world? Microsofto's Azure Core team is looking for a Software Engineer II to help us build the platform that runs customer virtual machines, networks and storage at global scale.

## Responsibilities
- Design, develop and test features for a distributed control plane that manages hundreds of thousands of servers.
- Troubleshoot and resolve production issues, participating in an on-call rotation with strong support from senior engineers.
- Write clear technical specs and contribute to design reviews.
- Collaborate with teams across Azure, including networking, security and compute.
- Embody our culture and values.

## Qualifications
Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 2+ years technical engineering experience with coding in languages including, but not limited to, C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python OR equivalent experience.
- Ability to meet Microsofto, customer and/or government security screening requirements are required for this role. These requirements include, but are not limited to, the following specialized security screenings: Microsofto Cloud Background Check.

Additional or Preferred Qualifications:
- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 4+ years technical engineering experience with coding in languages including C#, Java or Python.
- Experience with distributed systems or cloud platforms.
- Experience with containerization and orchestration technologies such as Kubernetes.
- Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
