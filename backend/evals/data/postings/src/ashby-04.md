## About the Team
The API Platform team builds the systems that serve our models to developers around the world: authentication, rate limiting, billing, quotas, and the public APIs themselves. Millions of developers rely on us, so reliability matters as much as speed.

## About the Role
We're looking for a Software Engineer to help scale our API platform. You'll work across the stack but mostly on Python services, designing new product surfaces and hardening the ones we have.

## In this role, you will
- Design and build backend services in Python that power our public API.
- Own features end to end, from design review through rollout and monitoring.
- Improve rate limiting, usage metering and billing correctness.
- Work with PostgreSQL and Redis at scale, and design data models for new products.
- Respond to incidents and make the systems more resilient.
- Collaborate with research and applied teams to launch new models safely.

## You might thrive in this role if you
- Have 4+ years of experience building backend systems in production.
- Are proficient in Python and have experience with web frameworks such as FastAPI.
- Have strong experience with relational databases.
- Have worked with queues, caches and asynchronous processing.
- Care about API design and developer experience.
- Have a humble attitude and eagerness to help colleagues.

## Nice to have
- Experience with Kubernetes and Terraform.
- Experience building usage-based billing systems.
