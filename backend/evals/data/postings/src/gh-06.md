## About Rampo
Rampo helps finance teams close the books faster, and automates the spend management work nobody wants to do. We're a 150-person company backed by top investors.

## The role
We're looking for a driven Account Executive to own a mid-market book of business. You'll run the full sales cycle, from first call to signed contract.

## What you'll do
- Source and close new business with finance leaders at companies of 200-2,000 employees.
- Run discovery, product demos and ROI conversations.
- Maintain an accurate pipeline in Salesforce.
- Partner with sales engineers and customer success on onboarding.

## What you'll bring
- 3+ years of full-cycle B2B SaaS sales experience.
- A track record of exceeding quota.
- Strong communication and negotiation skills.
- Experience selling to finance or accounting buyers is a plus.

## Compensation
OTE $180,000 - $220,000 (50/50 split), plus equity.

Rampo is an equal opportunity employer.
