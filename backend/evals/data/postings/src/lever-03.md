Retoolo is the fastest way to build internal software. Thousands of companies use us to build admin panels, dashboards and workflows on top of their own databases, and we're a small team where one engineer's work reaches a lot of users. We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer who loves Python and Postgres to join our Data Connectors team. Our backend is Python and FastAPI with PostgreSQL, Redis and Celery, deployed on AWS with Docker. You'll build the layer that securely connects our product to customers' databases and APIs, which means thinking a lot about performance, multi-tenancy, permissions and failure modes. Day to day you'll design new connector types, write the async workers that execute queries on behalf of users, tune slow SQL, and mentor two or three engineers on the team. We care about developer experience internally, so you'll also have time to improve our local environment and test suite. You don't need to have used our product, but you should be curious about how people build internal tools. We look for at least 5 years of backend experience, strong Python, deep PostgreSQL knowledge and comfort owning a service from design through on-call. Experience with Kubernetes is not needed, since our platform team handles that. Bonus points for experience with query planners, connection pooling or building multi-tenant systems. We pay $185k-$230k plus meaningful equity, cover health, dental and vision in full, and give every new hire a $3,000 setup budget. We're an equal opportunity employer and we'd love to hear from you even if you don't tick every box.
