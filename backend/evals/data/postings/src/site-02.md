Planetscalo is the MySQL platform for developers who need massive scale without the operational pain. We run on Vitess, the open source database clustering system that powers some of the largest sites on the internet.

## About the role
As a Database Engineer you'll work on the core of our product: the systems that orchestrate, scale and heal thousands of customer databases. You'll write Go, debug distributed systems and talk to customers about how their databases behave under pressure.

## Responsibilities
- Develop features in Vitess and our control plane, mostly in Go.
- Diagnose and fix performance and reliability problems in production databases.
- Contribute upstream to Vitess and engage with the open source community.
- Participate in a follow-the-sun on-call rotation.
- Write design docs and mentor teammates.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in backend or infrastructure engineering.
- Deep knowledge of relational databases, including query planning, replication and transactions. MySQL experience strongly preferred; PostgreSQL experience is welcome.
- Proficiency in Go, or the ability to become proficient quickly.
- Experience with distributed systems concepts such as consensus and sharding.
- Comfort with Linux, containers and Kubernetes.

## Nice to have
- Open source contributions to database projects.
- Experience running databases at scale on AWS or GCP.

## Compensation
$170,000 - $220,000 depending on level and location, equity, and a full benefits package. Four weeks of PTO.

## Interview process
Intro call, technical deep dive, systems design interview, and a conversation with our CTO. We'll ask you to explain a system you've built.

Planetscalo is an equal opportunity employer.
