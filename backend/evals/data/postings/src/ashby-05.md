## About Deelo
Deelo helps companies hire and pay people anywhere in the world. We're a remote-first company with employees in over 60 countries.

## The role
You'll own full-cycle recruiting for engineering and data teams, from intake to offer. You'll partner closely with hiring managers, and also help improve how we hire.

## Responsibilities
- Run full-cycle recruiting for engineering roles: intake, sourcing, screening, scheduling and closing.
- Build creative sourcing strategies to find candidates that others miss.
- Coach interviewers and hiring managers on structured interviews.
- Track pipeline metrics and report on them weekly.
- Provide a great candidate experience at every stage.

## Requirements
- 3+ years of technical recruiting experience, in-house or agency.
- Experience hiring software engineers and data scientists.
- Familiarity with an applicant tracking system such as Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever.
- Strong written communication and time management in an async environment.

## Bonus
- Experience with international hiring and compliance.
- Technical background.

## Pay
$85,000 - $120,000 USD with location-based adjustments, plus equity and flexible time off.

Deelo is an equal opportunity employer.
