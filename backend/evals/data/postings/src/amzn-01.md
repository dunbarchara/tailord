## Description
Amazono's Order Fulfillment Services team builds the systems that decide where, when and how every customer order is picked, packed and shipped. Every day we process hundreds of millions of events and our software has a direct effect on delivery speed for customers worldwide.

As an SDE II you will own the design and delivery of significant services, working with a small team of engineers and a product manager. You will be expected to dive deep, take ownership and deliver results, and to raise the bar for the engineers around you.

Key job responsibilities
- Design and build highly available, low-latency services on AWS
- Own operational excellence for your team's services, including on-call and root cause analysis
- Write well-tested, maintainable code in Java and contribute to design and code reviews
- Work with business and operations stakeholders to translate requirements into technical designs
- Mentor SDE I engineers and interns


A day in the life
You will start your day by reviewing dashboards and tickets, then join a standup. Most of your time is spent designing, coding and reviewing, with some time on-call. We value work-life harmony and flexible schedules.

## Basic Qualifications
- 3+ years of non-internship professional software development experience
- 2+ years of non-internship design or architecture (design patterns, reliability and scaling) of new and existing systems experience
- Experience programming with at least one software programming language

## Preferred Qualifications
- 3+ years of full software development life cycle, including coding standards, code reviews, source control management, build processes, testing, and operations experience
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent
- Experience with relational and NoSQL databases and distributed systems
- Knowledge of professional software engineering best practices
