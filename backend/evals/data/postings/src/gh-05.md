## Summary
Metao's Ranking team builds the models that decide what billions of people see each day. We're looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to help improve relevance, quality and integrity of our feed through large-scale deep learning.

## Responsibilities
- Develop, train and deploy deep learning ranking models using PyTorch on large GPU clusters.
- Design features, labels and offline evaluation to measure model quality, and run online experiments.
- Analyze results of A/B tests and communicate recommendations to product and leadership.
- Collaborate with infrastructure teams to improve training throughput and serving latency.
- Mentor other engineers and contribute to team direction.

## Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, a relevant technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 5+ years of experience in applied machine learning, with demonstrated use of deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with Python, C++ or Java.
- Experience with large-scale data processing and experimentation.

## Preferred Qualifications
- PhD in Machine Learning, Statistics or a related field.
- Publications at top machine learning conferences.
- Experience with recommendation or ranking systems.
- Experience optimizing GPU training or inference.

## Compensation
$177,000/year to $251,000/year + bonus + equity + benefits. Individual compensation is determined by skills, qualifications, experience and location. Compensation details listed in this posting reflect the base salary only, and do not include bonus, equity or sales incentives, if applicable.
