## About Anthropico
Anthropico's mission is to build reliable, interpretable AI systems that benefit society. Our team is a growing group of researchers, engineers, policy experts and business leaders working together on one of the most consequential technologies of our time.

## About the role
You will own the story and launch strategy for our developer platform: the APIs, SDKs and tools that thousands of engineering teams use to build with our models. You'll sit between product, engineering, sales and the developer community, translating technical capabilities into messaging that resonates.

## You may be a good fit if you
- Have 6+ years of product marketing experience, ideally for developer tools, infrastructure or API products.
- Can explain technical concepts to engineers and executives with equal credibility.
- Have run end-to-end launches, from positioning and pricing input through enablement and measurement.
- Are comfortable with data, and use it to decide what's working in your campaigns.
- Write exceptionally well.

## Strong candidates may also
- Have a technical background or have shipped code.
- Have experience working with open source or developer communities.
- Have launched products in a regulated or safety-sensitive space.
