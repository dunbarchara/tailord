Position Summary

What you'll do: Walmarto's Supply Chain Finance team partners with operations leaders to plan, forecast and improve the cost of moving goods from supplier to shelf. As a Senior Financial Analyst you will own forecasting and analysis for a major distribution network.

About the Team

Supply Chain Finance supports a network of more than 150 distribution centers and a fleet of thousands of trucks.

What You'll Do

  • Build and maintain monthly forecasts and annual budgets for assigned distribution centers.
  • Analyze variance to plan and explain drivers to operations leadership.
  • Develop financial models to evaluate network and automation investments.
  • Prepare executive presentations summarizing results and recommendations.
  • Automate recurring reporting using SQL, Excel and BI tools.

What You'll Bring

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or related area and 3 years' experience in financial analysis or forecasting, OR 5 years' experience in financial analysis.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • MBA or CPA.
  • Experience in supply chain or retail finance.
  • Advanced Excel and experience with SQL, Tableau or Power BI.
  • Strong communication skills.

Compensation Details

Compensation for this position is based on market data and may vary by location. Annual salary range: $80,000 - $160,000 per year in Bentonville, AR. Additional compensation includes annual bonus, stock and other benefits.

Benefits

Benefits: Beyond our great compensation package, you can receive incentive awards for your performance. Other perks include: 401(k) match, health benefits, financial wellness and mental health support, a Associate Discount, and PTO.
