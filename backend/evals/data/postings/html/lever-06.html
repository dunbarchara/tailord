Spotifo's ads business helps brands reach 600 million listeners. We are looking for a Customer Success Manager to guide our largest advertising customers to value.

What you'll do

  • Own the relationship with a portfolio of enterprise advertisers and agencies.
  • Understand each customer's goals and build success plans with clear metrics.
  • Run quarterly business reviews and present campaign performance.
  • Coordinate across sales, product and support to resolve issues quickly.
  • Spot expansion opportunities and hand them off to sales.

Who you are

  • 5+ years of experience in customer success, account management or consulting.
  • Experience working with enterprise customers in digital advertising or SaaS.
  • A strong analytical mindset and comfort with dashboards and spreadsheets.
  • Excellent written and spoken English; additional Nordic languages are a plus.
  • Ability to influence without authority.

Where you'll be

The role is based in Stockholm. Spotifo works in a hybrid model: our Work From Anywhere program lets you work from home or the office, including from another country for up to 60 days a year.

Benefits

Six months of paid parental leave, health and wellness benefits, and flexible holidays.

Spotifo is an equal opportunity employer. You are welcome at Spotifo for who you are, no matter where you come from, what you look like, or what's your job title.
