About us

Perplexito is building the world's best answer engine. We combine web-scale retrieval with large language models to give people direct, sourced answers. We're a small, intense team and we ship every day.

The role

Search Infrastructure owns the crawl, index and retrieval systems that every answer depends on. You'll make them faster, fresher and cheaper while the query volume doubles every few months.

Responsibilities

  • Design and build distributed backend services for indexing, retrieval and ranking.
  • Optimize latency and throughput of our retrieval stack, targeting p99 under 200ms.
  • Work on vector search, hybrid retrieval and embedding pipelines.
  • Build observability into every service and own the on-call for them.
  • Collaborate with ML engineers to productionize new retrieval models.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with at least some on high-scale systems.
  • Strong skills in Python, Go or Rust.
  • Experience with search engines, vector databases or information retrieval.
  • Experience operating services on Kubernetes in a cloud environment.
  • Strong ownership and a bias to action.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Elasticsearch, Vespa or similar.
  • Knowledge of ranking and learning-to-rank techniques.
  • Prior work on crawlers.

Compensation

$200,000 - $300,000 plus equity. We offer health, dental and vision, daily meals and a commuter benefit. We are in the office five days a week.
