Palantiro builds software that helps organizations integrate their data and make better decisions. Forward Deployed Software Engineers sit at the intersection of engineering and the real world, building solutions with customers in government, healthcare and industry.

A day in the life

  • Embed with customer teams to understand their hardest operational problems.
  • Build data pipelines, applications and models on top of our platform, often in the customer's own environment.
  • Ship in days, not months, and iterate based on user feedback.
  • Travel to customer sites up to 25% of the time.
  • Write code in Python, TypeScript and Java as needed.

Core qualifications

  • Strong programming skills in at least one language, and a desire to learn others.
  • Experience building software with real users and real constraints.
  • Comfort with SQL and data modeling.
  • Ability to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • U.S. citizenship and the ability to obtain a security clearance are required for this role.

What we value

  • A bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in a technical field.
  • Experience with cloud platforms and Docker.
  • Interest in problems that matter.

Benefits

We offer competitive salary of $120,000 - $190,000, equity, healthcare, parental leave and a relocation package.

Equal opportunity

Palantiro is committed to hiring a diverse workforce. We are an equal opportunity employer and do not discriminate on the basis of any protected characteristic. Candidates need to be authorized to work in the United States. Palantiro is not able to sponsor visas for this role.
