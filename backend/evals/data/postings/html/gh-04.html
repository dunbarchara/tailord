Backend Engineer, Python (Incident Management)

New York, NY, USA

About Datadogo

We're on a mission to bring high-quality monitoring and security to every engineering team. Datadogo is a SaaS platform trusted by tens of thousands of organizations, and our engineering teams ship to production hundreds of times a day.

The team

Incident Management helps on-call engineers detect, triage and resolve problems quickly. We build the APIs, background workers and integrations behind paging, status pages and postmortems.

At Datadogo, you will

  • Build and operate Python services that handle tens of thousands of requests per second.
  • Design PostgreSQL schemas and queries that stay fast as our customers' data grows.
  • Develop asynchronous workers on top of queues to deliver notifications reliably.
  • Write unit, integration and load tests, and be thoughtful about rollout and rollback.
  • Be part of an on-call rotation for the services you build.
  • Work with product designers and managers on new features.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of professional experience building backend services, preferably in Python.
  • Solid understanding of relational databases, especially PostgreSQL.
  • Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
  • Experience with a major cloud provider such as AWS.
  • Willingness to learn Go, which we use for latency-critical paths.

Bonus points

  • Experience with Kafka, Redis or other messaging systems.
  • Experience with Terraform or Kubernetes.
  • Contributions to open source projects.

Benefits and growth

  • A new hire comp package including equity
  • Continuous career development and an internal mobility program
  • Best-in-class benefits, including a generous parental leave and a new-hire stipend for equipment
  • Pay range: $138,000 - $186,000 USD

I understand and agree that both Datadogo and I waive our respective rights to trial by jury in connection with any claims covered by this agreement to arbitrate. The parties will select one neutral arbitrator, who will have the authority to award all relief in law or equity that is requested by the parties and supported by credible, relevant, and admissible evidence. Datadogo will bear the costs that are particular to the arbitration, such as the arbitrator fees. The arbitrator will determine the arbitration venue, based on fairness and convenience to the parties and witnesses, unless otherwise mutually agreed upon by the parties. The Party initiating a claim under this Agreement must make a written demand for arbitration to the other Party. The demand for arbitration shall identify the claims asserted, the facts upon which such claims are based, and any relief or remedy sought. Written demand for arbitration to Datadogo must be sent to Datadogo PBC at arbdemands@datadogo.com. Datadogo’s demand for arbitration shall be sent to the most recent mailing address and/or email address that Datadogo has on file for you. 

This agreement to arbitrate does not apply to claims for workers’ compensation, unemployment compensation benefits, claims or charges before any administrative agency having jurisdiction over such claims, or any other claim that is not subject to arbitration under federal law. I agree that any issues pertaining to the enforceability, application or validity of this agreement to arbitrate shall be decided only by a court of competent jurisdiction and not by an arbitrator. This agreement to arbitrate applies only in the United States.
