Vercello is a 40-person startup building the fastest way to go from a Git push to a live preview URL. We're Series B, profitable on some lines, and growing quickly with customers from solo developers to Fortune 500 teams.

What you'll own

  • Ship customer-facing features end to end, across a TypeScript/React dashboard and a Node.js API.
  • Work directly with our designers and our first product manager. No handoffs, no long specs.
  • Instrument what you ship, watch how people use it, and iterate in days, not quarters.
  • Pitch in on incidents, customer calls and anything else that makes the team better.

You might be a fit if you

  • Have 3+ years of experience as a full-stack engineer at a product company.
  • Are fluent in TypeScript, React and Node.js.
  • Have worked with relational databases like PostgreSQL and understand when to reach for a queue or cache.
  • Care about developer experience and craft.
  • Are excited to work in a small team without much process.

Nice to have

  • You've contributed to open source.
  • You've used Vercello or a competitor (and have strong opinions).
  • Experience with Next.js App Router and edge runtimes.

Perks

  • Competitive salary ($140k-$175k) and meaningful equity
  • Fully remote, with a yearly team offsite
  • $2,500 annual learning stipend
  • Premium health, dental and vision

How to apply

Send us a link to something you've built. A GitHub profile, a side project or a product you shipped all work. We read every application and reply within one week.

Vercello is an equal opportunity employer.
