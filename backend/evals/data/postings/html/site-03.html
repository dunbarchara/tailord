Senior Software Engineer, Developer Experience

Job ID: 1636481 | Applo

Apply now

Summary

Imagine what you could do here. At Applo, new ideas have a way of becoming extraordinary products, services and customer experiences very quickly. Bring passion and dedication to your job and there's no telling what you could accomplish.

Our Developer Experience team builds the internal tools that thousands of Applo engineers use to build, test and ship software every day. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to improve how our engineers work.

Description

In this role you will design and build developer tools, services and workflows. You'll work with teams across the company to understand pain points, and you'll ship improvements that save engineers time. This is a role for someone who enjoys building for builders.

Your responsibilities

Design and develop internal build, test and deployment services
Improve CI performance and reliability across large monorepos
Partner with product teams to roll out new tooling and migrate legacy systems
Define metrics for developer productivity and track them over time
Write clear documentation and lead trainings

Minimum Qualifications

7+ years of professional software development experience
Proficiency in one or more of Python, Swift, Go or Java
Experience with continuous integration systems and build tooling
Strong understanding of Git, code review and release engineering
Excellent communication skills

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with Bazel or similar build systems
Experience operating services on Kubernetes
Prior experience on a developer productivity team
BS, MS or PhD in Computer Science or equivalent experience
