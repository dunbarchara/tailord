About Linearo

Linearo is the issue tracker built for modern product teams. We're a remote team of 60 that believes in building software with care. Our customers love our speed and focus, and we want to keep raising the bar.

The role

We're hiring a Product Designer to own the experience of core workflows used daily by hundreds of thousands of people. You'll work in a small cross-functional team with engineers and a product manager, from the first sketch to the shipped pixel.

What you'll do

  • Design complete features end to end: research, flows, interaction details and visual design.
  • Prototype in code or in Figma to test ideas quickly with real users.
  • Contribute to our design system, and evolve its components as the product grows.
  • Partner with engineering to craft details such as motion, keyboard shortcuts and empty states.
  • Present your work clearly and incorporate critique from the whole company.

About you

  • 5+ years of product design experience, with a portfolio of shipped work on complex software.
  • Strong systems thinking, with an eye for hierarchy, typography and interaction.
  • Experience working closely with engineers and shipping iteratively.
  • Comfortable with Figma and with learning new tools.

Bonus

  • Experience designing for developers or other expert users.
  • You can code in HTML, CSS or React.

Compensation

$150,000 - $190,000 depending on experience, plus equity. We hire globally and adjust ranges by region.

Benefits

Four-day-ish weeks in summer, a home-office budget, annual team retreats, and the best keyboard we can find.

Linearo is an equal opportunity employer. We encourage candidates from all backgrounds to apply.
