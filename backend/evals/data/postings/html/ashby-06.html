About Cursoro

Cursoro is building the most useful AI coding companion. Our app is used by hundreds of thousands of developers, and we're launching our first native iOS experience.

What you'll do

  • Own the iOS app from architecture to App Store release.
  • Build polished, performant UI with SwiftUI and UIKit.
  • Integrate streaming responses from our backend into a fluid chat experience.
  • Set up testing, CI and release processes for mobile.
  • Work with design and backend engineers to ship every week.

What we're looking for

  • 4+ years of experience building iOS apps in Swift.
  • Strong understanding of SwiftUI, concurrency and networking.
  • A portfolio of shipped apps you're proud of.
  • Experience working in a small team with a lot of ownership.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Kotlin or Android.
  • Experience building apps for developers.

$170,000 - $240,000 plus equity. Health, dental and vision. Lunch and dinner at the office. We work in person in San Francisco.
