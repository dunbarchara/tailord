Mistralo builds frontier open-weight AI models and the products around them. We are a team of about 250 researchers and engineers, and we move fast while keeping our infrastructure and customers' data safe.

The mission

You will secure the systems that train and serve our models, from our GPU clusters to our API.

What you'll do

  • Design and implement security controls for cloud and bare-metal infrastructure.
  • Threat model new systems and review architecture with engineering teams.
  • Build detection and response capabilities, and lead incident investigations.
  • Run vulnerability management, penetration testing and our bug bounty program.
  • Drive SOC 2 and ISO 27001 work with our compliance lead.

About you

  • 5+ years in security engineering, application security or a related field.
  • Strong knowledge of Linux, networking and cloud security (AWS, GCP).
  • Experience with Kubernetes security, secrets management and IAM design.
  • Scripting ability in Python or Go.
  • Excellent judgment and communication, and a bias for pragmatic solutions.

Plus

  • Experience securing machine learning infrastructure.
  • Offensive security background.
  • Fluent French.

Package

€90,000 - €140,000, with equity. Visa support and relocation assistance for Paris. Meal vouchers and public transport reimbursement.

Mistralo is an equal opportunity employer.
